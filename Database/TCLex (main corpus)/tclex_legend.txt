COLUMN LEGEND
=============

lemma
    Lemma/base form of the word.

pos
    Part of speech.

corpus_frequency
    Total frequency of the lemma in the corpus.

spoken_frequency
    Frequency of the lemma in the spoken component.

written_frequency
    Frequency of the lemma in the written component.

literature
    Frequency of the lemma in the literature component.

meb
    Frequency of the lemma in the MEB (Turkish Ministry of National Education
    Textbooks) component.

saha
    Frequency of the lemma in the SAHA (interviews with primary school
    children) component.

tubitak
    Frequency of the lemma in the TÜBİTAK (Bilim Çocuk Journal by TÜBİTAK)
    component.

video
    Frequency of the lemma in the video component.

class
    Conceptual class assigned to the lemma.

subclass
    Conceptual subclass (more granular) assigned to the lemma.

num_docs
    Number of documents in which the lemma occurs.

mps
    Morphological Paradigm Size (i.e., the number of distinct wordforms
    associated with the lemma).

cd
    Contextual Diversity measure.

pmw
    Frequency per million words.

zipf
    Zipf frequency score.

length
    Length of the lemma in characters.

sc
    Syllable count, operationalized as the number of vowels in the lemma.
